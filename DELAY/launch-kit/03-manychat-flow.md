# ManyChat: the comment-to-DM flow

This is the single highest-leverage step in the whole funnel. Everything else is content; this is the
part that turns attention into an owned contact.

Build it once, test it end to end, then reuse the same trigger on every post.

---

## The trigger

**Type:** Instagram → Comment on a specific post/Reel (start with "any post", narrow later)

**Keywords:** `RESET`, `reset`, `Reset`

Add these as separate keyword entries. ManyChat's matching is more literal than you'd expect, and
people type in every casing imaginable. Also add the two most common near-misses: `RESTE` and
`RESET!`.

**Match type:** Contains, not Exactly. People write "RESET please 🙏" and you want them all.

---

## Step 1: Public comment reply (automatic)

ManyChat can post a public reply to the comment. Use it. It signals to everyone scrolling the
comments that the thing is real, and comment volume is a ranking signal.

Rotate between these three so the comment section doesn't read like a bot:

```
Sent. Check your DMs 🙌
```
```
On its way to your DMs now.
```
```
Just sent it over. It's in your messages.
```

---

## Step 2: The DM

Keep it to two messages. The first opens the conversation window, the second delivers.

**Message 1**

```
Hey, you asked for the reset, so here it is.

It's three pages: why arguments escalate in the first 90 seconds, four sentences you can actually say out loud, and a worksheet to fill in when things are calm.
```

**Message 2**, with a button rather than a bare link. Instagram suppresses bare links in DMs and buttons
convert considerably better.

```
Grab it here 👇
```

Button label: `Send me the reset`
Button URL: `https://aarondelaycounseling.com/reset/?src=ig`

---

## Step 3: The follow-up (24 hours later, conditional)

Only fires if they did **not** click the button. ManyChat can branch on this.

```
No pressure at all, just checking the link worked, they sometimes get eaten by Instagram.

Here it is again if you want it: aarondelaycounseling.com/reset
```

Do not send a third. One follow-up is helpful; two is a bot.

---

## Why a button and not the bio link

Because the bio link asks someone to leave the video, remember why they cared, tap a profile, tap a link, and land on a page, and most people don't complete that journey. The comment happens at the
peak of interest, and the DM arrives while they still remember what the Reel said.

It's also the reason to keep the keyword to one word. "Comment RESET" gets typed. "Comment the word
RESET below and I'll send it" gets read and ignored.

---

## Testing before you rely on it

Do this properly once, from a second account that does not follow Aaron:

1. Comment the keyword on a live post
2. Confirm the public reply appears
3. Confirm the DM arrives (and check the **Requests** folder, since DMs from non-followers land there)
4. Tap the button, land on `/reset/`, submit a real email address
5. Confirm the PDF actually arrives from Kit
6. Confirm the subscriber appears in Kit tagged `source: reset-landing`

Step 6 is the one people skip and it's the one that matters. If source tagging is broken you lose
the ability to tell church traffic from Instagram traffic, which is the data the whole ninety-day
read depends on.

---

## Caption pattern for every post

The Line goes first. The ask goes last. Nothing between them.

```
[THE LINE: the one sentence people screenshot]

[2–4 sentences of the Truth]

Comment RESET and I'll send you the free 3-page argument reset.
```
