# The insurance / compliance email

Written 2026-08-29, after the site went live. This **supersedes Note 2 in
`04-compliance-outreach.md`**, which is now out of date: it says the list lives in Kit (it's
MailerLite), it says nothing is being sold yet (the $19 PDF is live and taking payment), and it
describes coaching as something Aaron is "exploring" (the enquiry form is live and routes people).

Two pieces below. The first is Ben to Aaron. The second is written in Aaron's voice so he can
forward it to his carrier without rewriting it.

---

## 1. Ben → Aaron

**Subject:** One thing I need from you before we point traffic at the site

Aaron,

The site is finished and everything on it works. Before we start actually driving people to it,
there's one box I can't tick for you, because it needs somebody who carries risk on your license.

Underneath this note is a message written in your voice for your malpractice carrier's
risk-management line. Send it as it is, or change whatever you want, but it's meant to be
forwarded rather than rewritten.

Why now rather than later:

- The $19 PDF is live and can take money from anyone in the country.
- The coaching enquiry form is live and is aimed at people outside Tennessee.
- The disclaimer language on the site is mine. It's careful, but careful and approved aren't the
  same thing. I'd rather they hand you the wording they want. Swapping it everywhere takes me
  about ten minutes, so there's no cost to you in asking for exactly what you want.

One thing worth saying plainly, because it's the question they'll ask first: I have not touched
the practice's client list and I'm not going to. Nobody is on the email list except by their own
action on the website. There's no connection of any kind between the site and the EHR.

Send it whenever. Nothing's being promoted until you hear back.

Ben

## 2. Aaron → his malpractice carrier / healthcare attorney

**Subject:** Risk-management review: non-clinical coaching and digital products alongside my practice

Hi [name],

I'm Aaron Delay, LPC-MHSP, licensed in Tennessee. I run a counseling practice in Bartlett that
sees roughly ten thousand sessions a year.

I've built a personal-brand website that is separate from the practice, and I'd like your
risk-management read on how I've structured it. I'd rather have this reviewed now than retrofit
it later.

### What is live today at aarondelaycounseling.com

1. A free educational PDF, given in exchange for an email address.
2. A $19 educational PDF sold nationally through Gumroad.
3. A weekly educational email to people who opt in.
4. An enquiry form for **non-clinical marriage coaching**, delivered by video, aimed at people
   **outside** Tennessee.
5. An enquiry form for churches booking me to speak. No fee.
6. A page for a forthcoming book.

### How I've drawn the line

The site routes people by where they live, and says so in those words:

- **In Tennessee** they are directed to the practice for counseling: assessment, diagnosis where
  warranted, and treatment, booked and recorded through the practice.
- **Outside Tennessee** the only thing offered is coaching, described as non-clinical, with no
  diagnosis, no treatment, and no clinical record.

No current or former counseling client is solicited for any of it.

### The exact language currently on the site

In the footer of every page:

> Everything on this site is educational. It is not psychotherapy, diagnosis, or treatment, and
> reading it does not create a therapist–client relationship. Clinical counseling services are
> provided separately and only to residents of states where Aaron is licensed. If you are in
> crisis, contact a licensed provider in your state or call or text 988.

On the coaching page:

> Coaching is not therapy. No diagnosis, no treatment, no clinical record, and it does not create
> a therapist–client relationship. If what you need is treatment, I'd rather help you find it than
> sell you something adjacent to it.

On the paid PDF's sales page:

> This is an educational resource. It is not psychotherapy, it does not diagnose or treat
> anything, and buying it does not create a therapist-client relationship. If you're in crisis,
> contact a licensed provider in your state or call or text 988.

Next to the coaching enquiry form:

> This goes to Aaron directly. Please don't include anything you'd consider clinical detail. If
> you're in crisis or need urgent help, contact a licensed provider where you live, or in the US
> call or text 988.

**I wrote that language myself. Please treat it as a starting point, not as something I'm asking
you to bless.** If you'd rather it said something else, tell me what, and I'll change it
everywhere the same week.

### What I have deliberately not done

- I have not imported the practice's client contacts — roughly 3,500 people — into the mailing
  list, and I don't intend to. Nobody is added by me or my staff. People subscribe by their own
  action on the website.
- There is no connection between the mailing list system and the practice's EHR.
- I don't take coaching clients who are current or former counseling clients.
- I don't sell anything from the stage when I speak at a church, and I don't take counseling
  clients from the room.

### My questions

1. Does my current policy cover, exclude, or take a view on **non-clinical coaching delivered by
   video to people outside Tennessee**? If it's excluded, what's the right coverage and what does
   it cost?
2. Does selling **educational digital products nationally** raise anything you'd want handled
   differently?
3. Should the coaching sit in a **separate legal entity** from the practice, or is a separate
   brand and a written agreement enough?
4. Is there **wording you want** in a coaching agreement — for scope, for the referral-out
   trigger, for records?
5. Are there **Tennessee board rules on advertising** I should read before promoting this? I want
   to be careful about anything that reads as a testimonial or an outcome claim.
6. Does anything about **my licensure follow me** into non-clinical work in other states, or into
   states that regulate the word "coaching"?
7. Is there an **intake screen** you'd want in place before someone is accepted for coaching, to
   route clinical presentations to care instead of enrolling them?

I'm not promoting any of this until I hear back, so take the time you need.

Thanks,

Aaron Delay, LPC-MHSP
aaron@aarondelaycounseling.com

## Appendix: what I'd flag to Aaron separately

Two things I noticed while pulling the exact language together. Neither is urgent, both are worth
a sentence when you talk to him.

**1. The free PDF has no educational disclaimer on it.**
Every page of the website carries one. The Gumroad listing carries one. The free reset PDF — the
one that gets emailed out, handed round small groups and dropped into church newsletters, and so
travels further than anything else — carries only a safety line about abuse and addiction. It's
the most widely distributed asset and the only one without the disclaimer. Say the word and I'll
add the same sentence the site already uses and re-render it; it's about ten minutes.

**2. The practice is called two different things.**
The website says **Life Wellness Counseling**. The free PDF says **Life Wellness Clinic**. One of
them is wrong and I don't know which. Tell me the right one and I'll make it consistent.

Also minor, not compliance: the Gumroad storefront still shows the seller as **aarontherapy**,
which is the old account handle. Worth renaming to Aaron Delay Counseling so the checkout matches
the site people came from.
