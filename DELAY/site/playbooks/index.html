Questions

Before you buy

Is this counseling?

No. It's an educational resource. It isn't psychotherapy, it doesn't diagnose anything, and buying it doesn't make you my client. If you need clinical care, I'd rather you get it than read this.

Do both of us need to read it?

It works better with two, but it's written so one person can start alone. Several of the exercises are designed to be handed across the table rather than explained.

How is it different from the free reset?

The reset solves one moment: the ninety seconds when a conversation turns. The playbook deals with the pattern that keeps producing those moments, and what to do between them.

Is it religious?

No. It's written for anyone with a marriage. I'm a credentialed minister, so my values are visible in how I write, but nothing in the tools depends on you sharing them.

What if it isn't for me?

Email me and I'll refund it. I'd rather have the note than the nineteen dollars.
